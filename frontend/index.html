What MedRemind+ does

A small, practical medication tracking prototype.

Medicine schedule

Keep medicine names, dosage, instructions and reminder times together.

Simple reminders

Confirm a dose, postpone it, or test a reminder during a demo.

Adherence history

See taken, missed and postponed doses over time.

Caregiver view

A connected caregiver can see patient activity and send reminders.

How it works

The basic workflow behind the prototype.

1. Add a medicine

Enter medicine details and choose a fixed or interval-based schedule.

2. Get a reminder

A reminder modal appears when the medicine becomes due.

3. Confirm the dose

The patient records the dose as taken or postpones the reminder.

4. Caregiver stays informed

The caregiver dashboard uses shared prototype data to show activity.

MedRemind+ — Frontend prototype using HTML, CSS, JavaScript and localStorage.
